# Shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75; otherwise it costs $6.95. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.
Express shipping costs $14.95 and arrives in 1 to 2 business days when ordered before 2 PM Eastern.
Orders are processed within 1 business day. You receive a tracking link by email and WhatsApp as soon as the order ships.
We ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship internationally yet.
